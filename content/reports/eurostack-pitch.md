Europe has not developed an integrated indigenous digital ecosystem, and the spaces have been occupied
by large US corporations: not only services and applications, but the entire value chain that supports
them, from chips to data to compute to connectivity. This dependency is extractive, accelerating with
the AI build-out, and a front-line issue for security, resilience, and the economy.

This pitch document, dated 10 January 2025, sets out the EuroStack mission: to join and build the
logical and physical infrastructures that secure Europe’s role in competitive digital value chains, with
an urgent programme of action rather than yet more regulation.

Contributors include Robin Berjon, Cristina Caffarra, Francesco Bonfiglio, Vittorio Bertola, Sebastiano
Toffaletti, and Kai Zenner. The initiative continues at [eurostack.eu](https://eurostack.eu/).
