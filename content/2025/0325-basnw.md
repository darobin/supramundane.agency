This came as a huge and delightful surprise: _[We Need To Rewild The Internet](https://www.noemamag.com/we-need-to-rewild-the-internet/)_,
which I co-authored with the mighty [Maria Farrell](http://mariafarrell.com/) and that was
published in April 2024 by [Noema](https://www.noemamag.com/) has been selected to
feature in the classic anthology _Best American Science & Nature Writing 2025_.

The book will come out later in 2025. I am incredibly excited to hold a physical copy.
