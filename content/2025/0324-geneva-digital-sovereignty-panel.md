The [Graduate Institute](https://www.graduateinstitute.ch) hosted
[an event](https://www.graduateinstitute.ch/communications/events/digital-data-sovereignty-definitions-and-technologies-independence-and)
convened by [Emrys Schoemaker](https://www.linkedin.com/in/emrysschoemaker/) to discuss data sovereignty
and infrastructure with the wider UN community. The event opened with a keynote from
[Ambassador Kah](https://www.linkedin.com/in/muhammadoukah/), and was followed by a panel
moderated by [Mark Scott](https://www.linkedin.com/in/markscott82/) and featuring
interventions from [Neha Mishra](https://www.linkedin.com/in/neha-mishra-388a8514/),
[Konstantinos Komaitis](https://www.linkedin.com/in/konstantinoskomaitis/), and yours
truly.

We discussed the rising importance of digital sovereignty in global digital governance, and
more widely in geopolitics and trade.

This is part of a series of events on digital sovereignty. Several further ones will be
announced soon and reports are being prepared.
