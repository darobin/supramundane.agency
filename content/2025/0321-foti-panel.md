For years, social media has been dominated by a handful of companies, shaping how information flows,
harming our informational enviroment, and limiting innovation. The dangers have never been clearer.
But a new paradigm is emerging—one where open protocols, competition, and user choice drive the
future of social media.

This panel discussed the Future of Technology Institute (FoTI) whitepaper
“[Algorithmic Pluralism: Towards Competitive & Innovative Information Ecosystems](/docs/foti-on-economy-algorithmic-pluralism-report.pdf)”
which I coauthored with [Sherif Elsayed-Ali](https://www.linkedin.com/in/sherifea/), bringing
us together with [Marietje Schaake](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Marietje_Schaake), former MEP and
Stanford University fellow, and [Emily Liu](https://bsky.app/profile/emilyliu.me), Head of Special
Projects at Bluesky.
