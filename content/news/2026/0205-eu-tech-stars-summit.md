The [EU Tech Stars Summit](https://www.greens-efa.eu/en/article/event/eu-tech-stars-summit), hosted by the
Greens/EFA group in the European Parliament on 5 February 2026, brought together the European tech
ecosystem that champions the Union's core values of democracy, privacy, and transparency. After MEP David
Cormand's welcome I gave the opening keynote, ahead of panels on chips, AI, and cloud with OVHcloud, Q.ANT,
Open Fiber, and Multiverse Computing.
