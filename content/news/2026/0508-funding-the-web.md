The web is slow-motion collapsing for multiple reasons, but at the heart of it all is a little-known
system: the search/browser levy. Designed to fund browsers by taxing search engine revenue, it does put
high-quality browsers in the hands of billions free of charge — at the steep cost of an artificial search
monopoly, a monoculture that disorganises our shared information space, and the defunding of the rest
of the web, notably news media.

We need not eliminate the levy; we can reform it so that it works for the web and the people who use it.
*Funding The Web: From Cartel to Covenant* documents the system and lays out a full toolbox of
solutions. Read it at [ftw.fund](https://ftw.fund/).
