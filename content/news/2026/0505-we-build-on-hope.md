I gave the keynote at [PublicSpaces 2026](https://publicspaces.net/) in Amsterdam. The current state of
tech can feel disheartening: years of critical analysis and reform efforts have left us with a digital
sphere that seems worse than ever, Europe's leaders seem unable to formulate a clear strategy, and tech
monopolies have openly aligned with authoritarian power.

Yet as dark as the situation may be, it is also clearer than ever. The internet as it exists today is the
result of a political project outlined in the 1990s, and we can replace it with a better vision. In this
talk I outline a strategy to bring democracy to the internet, and point to projects that show what a
healthier, more hopeful digital sphere looks like. The recording is below.
