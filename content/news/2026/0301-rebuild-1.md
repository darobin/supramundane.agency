[Rebuild](https://www.rebuild.net/) is a twelve-month sprint to catalyse European social platforms:
three 48-hour gatherings across 2026 connecting the entrepreneurs, pioneers, investors, and digital
leaders building the next generation of social platforms, founded by Thomas Madsen-Mygdal with Margrethe
Vestager as patron. The first gathering took place in Copenhagen on 1–3 March 2026 with more than 200
participants; I was there, working on the question the whole sprint is organised around: how do we
rebuild European social platforms?
