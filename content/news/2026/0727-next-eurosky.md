Mathilde Saliou interviewed me for [*Next*](https://next.ink/) about [Eurosky](https://eurosky.social/),
the European social web project: what it offers people — a reusable identity across social networks and
apps of the ecosystem — and what it is really about, which is whether Europe can build an industrial
capacity for a good technological environment. When people talk about sovereignty they usually have one
model in mind, Silicon Valley's, where everything is vertically integrated from floor to ceiling. Eurosky
is a bet on a different one. In French.
