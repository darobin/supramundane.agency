The counter-disinformation community meets for #Disinfo2026 in Vilnius, Lithuania. I'm doing two sessions.

In **Unshitted**, I will take the audience on a journey of unshitting the internet and discovering "social media" that doesn't suck your soul, doesn't suck your data, doesn't suck at all.

I'll also be chairing a panel on **Trust and safety through decentralised platforms**, with Cathleen Berger and D. Scarnecchia.
