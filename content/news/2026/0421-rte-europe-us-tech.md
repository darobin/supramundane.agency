Ireland's national broadcaster picked up the question that has been working its way through European
capitals: should Europe wean itself off US tech? On RTÉ Radio 1's *Today with David McCullagh*, Dr Hazel
Murray, Chair of Cybersecurity at Munster Technological University, and I talked through what Europe's
dependence on American cloud, software, and platforms actually exposes it to, why the "kill switch"
worry is not far-fetched, and what building credible alternatives would take. Twelve minutes; the
recording is below.
