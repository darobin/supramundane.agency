The Assemblée nationale set up a *commission d'enquête* on France's structural dependencies and systemic
vulnerabilities in the digital sector and the risks they pose to the country's independence — the GAFAM
in the MAGA era, as its report puts it. On 10 March 2026 I was heard by the commission, in a round table
with [David Chavalarias](https://iscpif.fr/) (CNRS) and Maud Quessard (IRSEM), following the audition of
Henri Verdier. The recording is below.

The commission's [report](https://www.assemblee-nationale.fr/dyn/17/rapports/cenum/l17b3054-t1_rapport-enquete.pdf)
(n° 3054, tome I, 453 pages, registered on 8 July 2026; Philippe Latombe presiding, Cyrielle Chatelain
rapporteure) draws on the hearing in several places — on access to research, on the advertising market, on
the position of small and medium-sized businesses, and on the risks of the digital omnibus.
