[Local-First Conf](https://www.localfirstconf.com/) gathered the local-first software community in Berlin
on 12–14 July 2026. I took part in the panel *Data Ownership Beyond Local First*, moderated by Eileen
Wagner, with Paul Frazee (Bluesky), Boris Mann (Ink & Switch), and Zenna Fiscella (Scuttlebutt): keeping
data on your own device is a start, but ownership is about who governs the protocols, identities, and
infrastructure that data moves through. The recording is below.
