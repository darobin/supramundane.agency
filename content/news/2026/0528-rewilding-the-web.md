Kathryn Nave and Dave Ward convened *Rewilding the Web* at the University of Edinburgh on 28–29 May 2026,
a workshop inspired by the essay Maria Farrell and I wrote,
[We Need To Rewild The Internet](https://www.noemamag.com/we-need-to-rewild-the-internet/), asking what
theoretical biology and ecology can teach us about cultivating sociotechnical infrastructure that resists
capture by monopolising tendencies. Maria and I were both there; my talk tied the social, technological,
and ecological crises together — cybernetics as the science of the polycrisis, and why we should not let a
good polycrisis go to waste.

Anil Madhavapeddy wrote up the whole thing in
[Rewilding the Web: my workshop report from Edinburgh](https://anil.recoil.org/notes/rewilding-the-web-report),
from coopetition and biological variety to moderation seen through the lens of artisanal cheese.
