[Rebuild 2](https://www.rebuild.net/gatherings/rebuild-2/) took the sprint for European social platforms
to Helsinki on 30 August – 1 September 2026: keynotes from Margrethe Vestager, Christian Lindholm, and
Marko Ahtisaari, forty masterclasses by practitioners, a track for kids building platforms, and — this
being Helsinki — sauna. I took part, as I had in [Rebuild 1](/2026/0301-rebuild-1/) in Copenhagen. The
sprint closes in Paris on 15 December 2026, when Rebuild open-sources its frameworks and tools back to
the industry.
