The Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung's [European Data Summit 2026](https://www.kas.de/en/events/detail/-/content/european-data-summit-2026)
— *Enforce. Simplify. Build.* — spent two days in Berlin on whether Europe's digital rulebook is
delivering, how to simplify it, and what to build. On 15 April I gave the keynote *Building a thriving open
social web for Europe*, speaking for Eurosky, and then joined the panel *Digital Public Infrastructure —
the Social Media Issue*: if social media is infrastructure for democratic societies, Europe cannot leave
it to a handful of foreign platforms. The recording is below.
