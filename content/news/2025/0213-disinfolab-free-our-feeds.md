A month after [Free Our Feeds](/2025/0113-free-our-feeds/) launched, [EU DisinfoLab](https://www.disinfo.eu/)
asked me to explain it to their community of disinformation researchers. With Joe McNamee moderating, I
laid out why the algorithms that shape our feeds produce polarisation by design, how open protocols
change the picture, and what a path to public interest social media infrastructure looks like. The
recording is below.
