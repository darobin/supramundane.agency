The BBC's [What's next for social media?](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c3w1e3xd2glo) surveys the
start-ups looking to capitalise on dissatisfaction with the social media giants. Speaking as one of the
nine custodians of [Free Our Feeds](https://freeourfeeds.com/), I make the case that Bluesky's open
protocol is a real opening — but that so long as one company is the primary operator of the
infrastructure, the risk remains that it will stop operating in the public interest.
