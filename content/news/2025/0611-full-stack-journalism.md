[Full Stack Journalism](https://www.linkedin.com/company/full-stack-journalism/), a visual podcast from Are
We Europe recorded live at Publix in Berlin, looks at the tension of newsrooms holding to account the very
platforms they rely on to do their work. For episode two, *Interfaces and influence: Reclaiming Newsroom
Technology*, host Sabrina Faramarzi had Sara Cooper, Tina Lee, and me dig into the hidden influences
underpinning the tech we use every day. The recording is below.
