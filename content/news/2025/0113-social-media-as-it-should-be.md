If our traditional media landscape featured only a handful of owners, we would call it what it is: a
threat to democracy. Yet that is where social media stands. In this *Project Syndicate* column, I explain
how the Bluesky model — an open protocol that anyone can build on, rather than a platform that one company
controls — could restore the original promise of the internet: a shared public space, governed in the
interest of the people who use it.
