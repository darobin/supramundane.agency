The BBC's [Should Europe wean itself off US tech?](https://www.bbc.com/news/articles/c3dpr2zkny0o) looks
at Europe's reliance on Amazon, Microsoft, and Google for the bulk of its cloud computing, and at the
digital sovereignty push in Brussels. I'm quoted on why concerns over a US “kill switch” deserve to be
taken seriously — and on the EU dragging its feet: there is political interest, but it has yet to be
turned into a shared strategy.
