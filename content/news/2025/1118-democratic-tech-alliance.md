The [Democratic Tech Alliance](https://democratic.technology/) is a coalition of members of the European
Parliament from different political groups, working with civil society, think tanks, experts, and
European businesses to create an EU tech ecosystem that supports democratic values and the public
interest: strategically resilient, decentralised, human-centred, interoperable, and enforced. It launched
in the European Parliament on 18 November 2025 with *Boosting Digital Democracy: How to create a
Democracy Shield that delivers*, organised with the European Partnership for Democracy and Nets4Dem and
backed by the S&D, Renew, and EPP groups.

I am a member of the Alliance's steering committee. Its first Assembly met in Brussels on 13 May 2026.
