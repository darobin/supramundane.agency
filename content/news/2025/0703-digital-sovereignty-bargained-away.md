With a leaked memo from the US–EU trade talks suggesting Brussels might water down tech enforcement in
hopes of lower tariffs, Cori Crider and I wrote in
[*POLITICO Europe*](https://www.politico.eu/article/digital-sovereignty-us-brussels-belgium-trade-talks-tech/)
that this would be a serious error, strategically and politically — polling shows strong majorities of
Europeans want the EU’s digital laws enforced even if it upsets Trump. What Europe needs is a coordinated
play: *break* entrenched monopolies, starting with Google; *enforce* the Digital Markets Act with the
resources it deserves; and *build* shared infrastructure for search and social media, funded in part by
what is reclaimed from the monopolies.
