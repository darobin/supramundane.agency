The Digital Summit in Gdańsk on 17–18 June 2025 was the closing event of the Polish Presidency of the
Council of the EU: ministers, MEPs, industry, researchers, and civil society at the European Solidarity
Centre, taking stock of cybersecurity, infrastructure resilience, technological sovereignty, and strategic
investment. I took part in *Towards European technological sovereignty and resilient digital public
sphere: challenges, opportunities & milestones*, co-hosted by the Panoptykon Foundation, Mozilla, the
Future of Technology Institute, Open Future, and the Open Markets Institute.
