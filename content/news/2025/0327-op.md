I had the pleasure of visiting the [Originator Profile](https://originator-profile.org/en-US/)
project in Tokyo. OP is a project bringing together a large group of media companies to develop
ways of marking content as authentic.

We discussed various aspects of disinformation and how to label content in ways that
respect pluralism and help avoid disinformation at the same time. We also looked at how
labelling works on [Bluesky](https://bsky.app/), atop the [AT Protocol](https://atproto.com/).

Additionally, it was great to get a chance to visit the [Yomiuri Shimbun](https://www.yomiuri.co.jp/).
