[AI Renaissance](https://airenaissance.org/) is a small, curated unconference on the political economy of
AI, organised by Metagov with the support of the royal family of Schaumburg-Lippe at Bückeburg Palace,
an hour outside Hanover. Some thirty-five researchers, builders, entrepreneurs, politicians, and artists
spend a week and a half challenging each other's assumptions and building new infrastructure, business
models, and institutions for public, open-source, and decentralised AI. I took part in the 2025 edition —
the same circle of people with whom the [Airbus for AI](/reports/airbus-for-ai/) policy brief took shape.
