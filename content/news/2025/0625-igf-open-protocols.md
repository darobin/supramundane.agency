The twentieth [Internet Governance Forum](https://www.intgovforum.org/en/dashboard/igf-2025) was hosted by
Norway in Lillestrøm on 23–27 June 2025. Alongside it, the Digital Public Goods Alliance and Project
Liberty Institute convened a workshop at NORAD on *Open Protocols and Public Interest Social Media* —
how open protocols can become digital public goods and what it takes to fund and govern public-interest
social media infrastructure. I spoke there, with participants from ICANN, Mastodon, Free Our Feeds, the
Social Web Foundation, the OpenID Foundation, and the University of Ottawa.
