Europe has spent a decade regulating US platforms while letting them occupy its entire digital value
chain. With Cristina Caffarra, Francesco Bonfiglio, Vittorio Bertola, Sebastiano Toffaletti, Kai Zenner,
and others, I contributed to the pitch document that launched [EuroStack](https://eurostack.eu/): a
programme to join and build the logical and physical infrastructures — from chips to data to compute to
connectivity — that Europe needs to secure its role in competitive digital value chains.
