Within weeks, outages at AWS and then Azure took down services across Europe — a reminder that the
continent runs on infrastructure it neither owns nor controls. In this *Project Syndicate* column, I
argue that the EU cannot reach technological sovereignty while leaving the entrenched US cloud monopolies
in place, and that dismantling them is a matter of resilience and security, not just competition policy.
