I'm delighted to be speaking at [Re:publica](https://re-publica.com/),
where I'll be giving a talk about "_[How We Can Finally Make The Digital World Democratic: Starting With Social Media](https://re-publica.com/de/session/how-we-can-finally-make-digital-world-democratic-starting-social-media)_".

The abstract says it all:

> Most of the digital sphere is under authoritarian governance: we only get change by begging large
> companies to act better or sometimes by forcing them. And when you take an authoritarian system and
> you use it as infrastructure for society, through the power of infrastructure that society becomes
> authoritarian as well.
>
> We cannot continue this way, but the fact that there is limited experience with democratic governance
> in the digital sphere makes it challenging to understand how to approach the problem. In this talk,
> we will see how by using open protocols and technical architectures that are compatible with
> democracy, and by understanding the role of digital infrastructure, we can build democratic social
> media.

See you there if you're going!
