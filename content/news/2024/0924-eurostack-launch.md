On 24 September 2024 a cross-party group of MEPs from the EPP, S&D, Renew, and the Greens hosted *Toward
European Digital Independence: Building the EuroStack* in the European Parliament, curated by Francesca
Bria, Cristina Caffarra, and Meredith Whittaker. Industry, technologists, institutions, think tanks, and
civil society gathered around one idea: that Europe needs a European industrial policy for its digital
infrastructure, from connectivity to cloud, AI, and platforms. I was one of the speakers. The
[EuroStack](https://eurostack.eu/) initiative grew out of that day; its
[pitch document](/reports/eurostack-pitch/) followed in January 2025.
