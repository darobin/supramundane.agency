**Supramundane Agency** is a consultancy specialised in the governance of
technology, operated principally by [Robin Berjon](https://berjon.com/).

Our methods include specifying protocols, developing libraries, prototypes, and products,
creating strategies to ensure that a specific technology has governance affordances,
designing institutions and governance mechanisms, participating in standards setting,
developing policymaker positions and white papers, and writing about technology for both
lay and specialised audiences.

If you're trying to define or deploy technology that isn't dystopian, that promotes
democracy and human agency, then you've come to the right place.
