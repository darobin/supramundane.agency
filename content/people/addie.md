Addie Wagenknecht has worked on infrastructure and its governance for two decades, including as Director of Global Ecosystems and Venture at Algorand Technologies, on network research at Protocol Labs and as Chair of the Open Source Hardware Association. She currently leads frontier work on secure compute environments within air-gapped systems. She is also an artist, founder of the open research collective Deep Lab with work in the collection of the Whitney Museum of American Art.
