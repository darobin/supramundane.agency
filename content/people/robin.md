Robin Berjon has worked on technology and governance for over twenty years, including as
Vice President of Data Governance at The New York Times, as editor of the HTML specification
at the W3C, and on decentralised systems at Protocol Labs. His work focuses on making the
digital sphere compatible with democracy: open protocols, institutional design, and the
governance of infrastructure.
