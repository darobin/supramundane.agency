Is it possible to get away from algorithms designed to generate polarised content? Social media is
changing fast, with open protocols and new products finally bringing about the promise of change. In this
[EU DisinfoLab](https://www.disinfo.eu/) webinar, moderated by Joe McNamee, I walk through how open
protocols open the door to public interest social media infrastructure and a much healthier information
environment — and what [Free Our Feeds](https://freeourfeeds.com/) is doing about it. 57 minutes.
