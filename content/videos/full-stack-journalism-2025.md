Full Stack Journalism is a visual podcast series from Are We Europe, recorded in front of a live audience,
that maps the “full stack” of tools journalists use and asks what a more ethical and transparent tech
standard could look like. In episode two, Sabrina Faramarzi hosted Sara Cooper, Tina Lee, and me for an
hour on the hidden influences built into the interfaces and infrastructure newsrooms rely on, and how they
might reclaim their technology.
