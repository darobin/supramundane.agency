The seventh [European Data Summit](https://www.kas.de/en/european-data-summit) was held at the Academy of
the Konrad-Adenauer-Stiftung in Berlin on 14–15 April 2026 under the motto *Enforce. Simplify. Build.* On
the second day I gave the keynote *Building a thriving open social web for Europe* and then joined the
panel *Digital Public Infrastructure — the Social Media Issue*. The recording is the full second day; it
starts at my keynote (3h19m in), with the panel following at 3h33m.
