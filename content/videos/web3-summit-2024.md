[Web3 Summit](https://web3summit.com/) is an open, community-programmed gathering for advancing a
functional, user-friendly decentralised web; the 2024 edition was held at Funkhaus in Berlin on
19–21 August. Speaking for IPFS, I made the case for a public interest internet: infrastructure that
is governed for the people who depend on it, and what decentralised technologies have to offer — and
still owe — to that goal. 25 minutes.
