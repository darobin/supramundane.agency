Local-first software puts people's data on their own devices, but ownership is a bigger question than
where the bytes live. On this [Local-First Conf 2026](https://www.localfirstconf.com/) panel, moderated by
Eileen Wagner, I discussed it with Paul Frazee (Bluesky), Boris Mann (Ink & Switch), and Zenna Fiscella
(Scuttlebutt). 45 minutes.
