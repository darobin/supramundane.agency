Radio, not video: David McCullagh had Dr Hazel Murray, Chair of Cybersecurity at Munster Technological
University, and me on RTÉ Radio 1's *Today* programme to discuss whether Europe should wean itself off US
tech — the risks of depending on a handful of American providers for cloud, software, and platforms, and
what a credible European alternative would take. Twelve minutes, in English. The player is RTÉ's own;
the clip is also on [rte.ie](https://www.rte.ie/radio/radio1/clips/22602926/).
