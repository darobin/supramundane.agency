[ATmosphereConf](https://2026.atmosphereconf.org/) is the global AT Protocol community conference; the
2026 edition was held at UBC in Vancouver on 26–29 March. On the Sunday I presented twice: the *WebTiles
Showcase* with [Ted Han](https://bsky.app/profile/knowtheory.net), and a lightning talk on *Matadata*,
publishing scientific data straight to the Atmosphere. Both were streamed live on Streamplace.
